About LA VAGUE Bottoms

From structured utility cargos to relaxed streetwear trousers and denim, LA VAGUE bottoms are designed in Lagos and crafted in Nigeria to pair cleanly with our oversized tees and heavyweight hoodies. Every silhouette focuses on durability, clean proportions, and everyday movement.

Unlike our oversized tops, LA VAGUE bottoms run true to size. Check the product size guide for waist and inseam measurements before placing your order. Paid orders are normally prepared within one business day for delivery across Nigeria, with free standard shipping on orders over ₦150,000.

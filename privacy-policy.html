LA VAGUE is a Nigerian streetwear store. This policy describes the data we actually use to run la-vague.store, process orders, provide support, secure the storefront, and operate the services behind it.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies when you browse LA VAGUE, contact us, place or track an order, use a discount code, join a product waitlist, submit a review, or otherwise interact with the store.

For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, LA VAGUE determines why and how customer personal data is used for the operation of the store.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Orders and checkout

When you place an order, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact data: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Delivery data: shipping address, city, state, postal code, and selected delivery method.
  • Order data: products, sizes, colours, quantities, prices, discount use, totals, order status, and timestamps.
  • Payment records: payment method, payment status, and the transaction reference needed to verify the order.

LA VAGUE does not store your full card or bank account details. Paystack handles the payment interface and payment credentials.

2.2 Support and store features

If you contact us, join a waitlist, or submit a review, we process the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, product or variant, rating, review text, and any review photos you submit.

2.3 Technical and security data

Our systems may process IP address, browser and device information, request metadata, security events, and application error/performance information. We use this information to operate, protect, diagnose, and improve the store.

3. How We Use Data

We use personal data only where there is a lawful reason to do so. Depending on the activity, our reasons include:

  • Contract: creating and fulfilling orders, taking payment, arranging delivery, sending transactional order emails, and providing order support.
  • Legal obligations: keeping records where required, responding to lawful requests, and meeting applicable consumer, tax, accounting, and data-protection duties.
  • Legitimate interests: fraud prevention, abuse prevention, service reliability, debugging, security monitoring, inventory control, and protecting the store and its customers.
  • Consent: where consent is specifically required, including any non-essential cookies, tracking, or direct marketing we may introduce in the future.

LA VAGUE does not currently operate a customer newsletter or advertising-profile programme through the storefront.

4. Payments and Order Processing

Payments are processed through Paystack. LA VAGUE sends the information needed to initialise and verify a transaction, then records the resulting payment status and reference against the order.

We use server-side payment verification before treating an order as paid. Payment card or bank credentials entered into Paystack are handled by Paystack rather than stored in the LA VAGUE database.

5. Service Providers and Disclosures

We use service providers to run the store. They may process limited data on our behalf or as required to provide their service:

  • Paystack: payment processing and verification.
  • Aiven: production PostgreSQL database hosting.
  • Render: backend API hosting.
  • Netlify: storefront hosting and delivery.
  • Brevo or configured email provider: transactional order and support email.
  • Cloudinary: product and review-image media hosting where used.
  • Sentry: application error and performance monitoring.
  • Delivery providers: information reasonably necessary to deliver an order.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect legal rights, investigate fraud or abuse, or respond to a valid request from a competent authority. We do not sell customer personal data.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

LA VAGUE currently uses a small amount of browser storage for core store functions:

  • CSRF security cookie: a first-party security token used to protect state-changing requests such as checkout actions.
  • Cart and wishlist storage: browser local storage keeps the products you add on your device.
  • Storage-notice acknowledgement: local storage remembers that you have dismissed the browser-storage notice.

We do not currently deploy Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or advertising cookies on the storefront. If we add non-essential analytics, advertising, or similar tracking in the future, we will update this policy and request consent before enabling it where required.

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing them may remove your cart, wishlist, or require a fresh security token.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including order fulfilment, customer support, accounting and legal requirements, fraud prevention, dispute handling, and security.

Different records can have different retention periods. We may retain limited transaction records where the law or a legitimate business need requires us to do so, while deleting or anonymising data that is no longer needed.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request information about our processing of your personal data, access it, correct it, request deletion or restriction, object to certain processing, request portability, withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis, and lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To make a privacy request, email support@la-vague.store or use our Contact page. We may need to verify your identity before providing, changing, or deleting personal data so that another person cannot make a request using your email address.

9. International Processing

Some infrastructure and service providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where applicable, we use those services subject to the data-transfer and safeguard requirements that apply to the processing.

10. Security

We use safeguards designed for the current store architecture, including HTTPS, access controls, CSRF protection, server-side payment verification, restricted admin routes, parameterised database queries, security logging, and redaction of sensitive request data from application logs and monitoring where configured.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a personal-data breach, we will assess and handle it in accordance with applicable notification duties.

11. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the store, service providers, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@la-vague.store or use our Contact page.
